Joukowsky Dynamics

A road car whose four wheels become ducted proprotors. Each wheel is a tyre on a duct rim with a 7-blade rotor inside; to fly, the car stands on landing legs and each wheel swings 90° up and outward on a hinge. Every part is modelled as a CAD solid, and the rotor blades are designed with the equations from NASA's Fundamental Proprotor Design Considerations.

  • CadQuery / OpenCascade
  • Three.js
  • Rotor aerodynamics
  • Momentum theory
  • Holographic sequence: watch the car assemble, drive, stand on its legs, open its petals, swing its wheels up into rotors and take off
  • Click any region (a wheel, the battery, the cockpit) to fly in and see it pulled apart, with a live briefing of what it's doing at that moment
  • 242 unique parts and 3,607 placed pieces, each with a datasheet, a three-view drawing and its own STEP file
  • Bill of materials, mass roll-up and centre of gravity computed from the CAD
  • Rotor calculator: blade twist and chord, stall margins, edgewise flow, power vs airspeed and hover endurance
  • Honest numbers: the analysis shows where the design falls short and what would fix it

Tideline

A radar satellite built to watch ports and shipping lanes. It uses synthetic-aperture radar (SAR) at X-band, so it sees ships through cloud, rain and darkness. A 4.1 m folding phased array with 680 transmit / receive modules images a berth at under 1 m or a 120 km stretch of shipping lane. On board it finds the ships, checks them against their AIS position reports to flag dark vessels, and sends alerts about blocked channels or anchorage queues within minutes.

  • CadQuery / OpenCascade
  • Three.js
  • Radar engineering
  • Orbital mechanics
  • Holographic mission: separation, solar wing and radar wings unfolding, a roll to point the beam, imaging a port, and the ship list going down
  • 44 unique parts and 1,547 placed pieces, every module, hinge and battery cell modelled, each with a datasheet and STEP file
  • Radar and mission physics calculated live: noise floor, ambiguities, smallest detectable ship, revisit, power, data, slewing and propellant, with 27 pass / fail checks
  • Port analytics: a simulated SAR image of a port where a real detector finds ships, matches AIS and spots congestion and blockages
  • A tabletop prototype you can build for about $400: a 60 GHz radar on a 1 m rail that images a model harbour through a cardboard “cloud”
