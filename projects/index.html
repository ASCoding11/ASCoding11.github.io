Hype Cycle Exchange

A live, multiplayer stock-market game for a classroom. The host runs timed trading rounds from a projector while everyone trades on their own phone or laptop with a game code. Each round reveals a year of news that moves the prices, and one company doesn't make it.

  • JavaScript
  • Firebase
  • Firestore rules
  • SVG charts
  • Web Audio
  • Daily Challenge: a new solo market every day, the same for everyone, with shareable scores and streaks
  • Buy, sell and short six stocks, including an index fund
  • Timed rounds, hidden results and a live leaderboard
  • Capital gains taxes: short-term vs long-term, with loss carryforward
  • News and Markets tabs with charts of every stock
  • A game-show podium finale with music and sound effects
  • Decades Edition: real companies from 1980–2008 in disguise, with real economic briefings each round and their true names revealed at the end

Players join with a 5-letter code from the host. Hosting needs a secret results file (one for the AI Hype edition, one for the Decades Edition), which isn't posted here.

Boxed

A competition math game built on real AMC 10 and AIME problems. Solve a new set of five every day, practice at the difficulty you choose, or host a live round for a math team where everyone answers on their own phone.

  • JavaScript
  • Firebase
  • MediaWiki API
  • Daily five: the same five problems for everyone, climbing from AMC 10 #1–5 to #21–25, with shareable scores and streaks
  • About 1,000 problems: AMC 10 (2010–2025) and the first five AIME problems (2010–2026), checked against the official answer keys
  • Difficulty bands (AMC 10 #1–5 up to the AIME) and four topics (Algebra, Geometry, Number Theory, Counting & Probability) that the host can switch before every problem
  • Answer pads drawn like the real bubble sheets, including the three-digit AIME grid
  • Live leaderboard with a speed bonus and a first-right-answer bonus, answer breakdowns after each problem and a final podium
  • Every answer links to full solutions on the AoPS Wiki
