Contact

Get in touch

Found a bug, have an idea for a game or tool, or have a question about the site? Send a message here and Arjun will read it.

Send a message

Leave an email address if you'd like a reply. Without one, your message still arrives, but there's no way to answer it.

Please don't include passwords or anything private.

Thanks, your message was sent. If you left an email address, you'll hear back there.

Other ways to reach the site

  • Deleting your data. To have your saved sign-in record or game stats deleted, send a message above from the email you signed in with, or include it in the message. The privacy policy explains what is saved.
  • Code and contributions. The site's code is on GitHub at ASCoding11/ASCoding11.github.io, where you can see how it's made and suggest changes.

What happens to your message

Messages are encrypted in your browser before they're sent, and stored in Google Firebase where only the site owner and admins can open them. They're used only to read and reply to what you sent. See the privacy policy for details.
