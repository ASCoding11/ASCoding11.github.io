How to play
  1. Point where you want to go and the plane follows your aim. The ring is your aim, the amber cross is where the guns point.
  2. Put the amber diamond on the cross and fire: it shows where to shoot so the bullets meet the target. Guns overheat, so fire in bursts.
  3. Hold an enemy near the middle until the ring says LOCKED, then launch a missile.
  4. When MISSILE flashes, drop a flare or break hard and barrel roll as it arrives.
  5. Fly through the floating rings: green repairs, orange is missiles, white is flares.
  6. Climbing costs speed. Too slow and you stall: nose down, throttle up. The ground, the water and the cliffs are always fatal.
