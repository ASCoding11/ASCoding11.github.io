How to play

  1. The marble rolls by itself and keeps speeding up. You only steer: A and D or the arrow keysdrag left and right anywhere.
  2. It's heavy. Steering pushes it, it doesn't snap. Curves pull it outward unless the deck leans in.
  3. Stay on the stone. Roll off an edge, or touch anything dark and iron, and the run is over.
  4. Blue gems and close calls raise your multiplier. Every 500 m is a milestone, and new marbles unlock as you go further.
