How to play

  1. The ball drops by itself down a steep skyway, faster the steeper it gets. You only steer: A and D or the arrow keysdrag left and right anywhere.
  2. It's heavy. Steering pushes it, it doesn't snap. Curves pull it outward unless the deck leans in.
  3. Stay on the deck. Cyan edges are drops. Go over one, or touch anything striped in hot amber, and the run is over. Magenta rails are safe walls.
  4. Pink shards and close calls raise your multiplier. Cyan chevrons fling you faster; amber bars brake you.
