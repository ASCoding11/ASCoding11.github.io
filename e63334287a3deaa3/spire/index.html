How to play
  • Tower: a stack of random sections. Climb the truss between sections to reach the next one. The clock runs twice as fast once someone reaches the top.
  • Glowing red means lethal. Falling isn't: you just land lower down.
  • Obby: 49 stages over seven worlds. Every stage starts on a checkpoint, and your furthest stage is saved.
  • Controls: WASD moves (relative to the camera), Space jumps (hold to keep jumping), the mouse turns the camera, the wheel zooms, Shift locks the camera behind you.
  • Touch: the stick moves, drag anywhere else to turn the camera, pinch to zoom.

Obby

Pick a stage you've reached. Checkpoints save as you go.

Practice

Every tower section on its own, as many tries as you like. A fall drops you back to the floor, and the clock shows your time.
