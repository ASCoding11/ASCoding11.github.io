Can't read feedback

Firestore said permission denied. Either the feedback block hasn't been added to your Firestore rules yet, or you're signed in with a different Google account from the one in those rules.

Copy the rules block from the e63334287a3deaa3/feedback.js entry in the repo's README.md, put your email in it (the same email check as your analytics rules), paste it into Firebase console → Firestore Database → Rules, publish, then press Refresh. Until it's added, nothing players send is saved.
