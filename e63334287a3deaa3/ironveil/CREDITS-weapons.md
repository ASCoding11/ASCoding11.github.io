# Midnight Ops weapons: credits

All weapon models, textures and materials in `assets/weapons.glb` were made for this game from code
(`tools/weapons/`). No outside models, textures, photos or scans are used. The designs are generic period-style
small arms built from public general dimensions; there are no maker's names, logos, proof marks, emblems or national
or political symbols anywhere on the models or in the textures.

Tools used to make them (not shipped with the game): Blender (GPL) as a Python module, gltf-transform (MIT),
meshoptimizer (MIT), sharp (Apache-2.0).
