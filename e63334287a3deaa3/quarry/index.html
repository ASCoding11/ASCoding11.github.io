Quarry

An endless world of hills, forests, deserts and snowy peaks, made of blocks you can dig out and build with. Craft tools at a crafting table, smelt ore in a furnace, raise animals for food and hold out against the zombies at night.

Survival: punch a tree for logs, turn them into planks, make a crafting table and lay out a wooden pickaxe in its 3×3 grid. Stone tools mine iron, iron tools mine gold and diamonds. Smelt ore and cook meat in a furnace with coal or wood. Eat to keep your hunger bar up, and get somewhere bright before night, when zombies come out.
