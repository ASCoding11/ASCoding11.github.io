Survival: punch a tree for logs, turn them into planks and a workbench, then make a wooden pickaxe. Stone tools mine iron, iron tools mine gold and azurite. Smelt ore in a kiln. Fall too far, stay underwater too long or touch lava and you'll lose hearts.
