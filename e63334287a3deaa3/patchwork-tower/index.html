Arrows move · Space fix · P pause

HOW TO PLAY

Hop Rivet the repair bot from window to window and press FIX until each one glows. Dark windows need two fixes. Fixing roots you to the spot.

The gargoyle on the roof kicks bricks down the column it stands over. When its eyes go red, a red arrow marks every column about to be hit.

Bats cross the rows: an arrow at the edge shows where one is coming from.

Grab the cocoa for a few seconds of invincibility and one-tap repairs. Planters block climbing and shutters block side steps.

Finish a floor quickly for a bigger bonus.
