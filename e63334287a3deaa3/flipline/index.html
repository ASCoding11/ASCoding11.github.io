How to play
  • One button: Space (or the up arrow, W, or the mouse).touch and hold anywhere on the screen. On the ground, holding is the gas; let go to coast.
  • In the air, holding flips you backwards. Let go and the spin slows down and the heavy front end drops the nose, so let go in time to land on your wheels.
  • Land on your wheels, close to the slope of the ground. Nearly level is a clean landing: it keeps your combo going and multiplies the next trick.
  • Red and cream with teeth means danger: spikes, spike ceilings, glowing posts, floating triangles, the swinging mace, saws, crushers, low beams and laser gates. Gold rounds are coins; a gold ×2 doubles your trick points for a while.
  • The run multiplier (the badge by your score) multiplies every trick and gem. Loops raise it most, clean landings and flips raise it a little, a gold crown at the top of a loop adds +0.5, and flips from a lit flip zone count three times. A sloppy landing halves it.
  • Gems sit inside loops, over big jumps and under ceilings: some can only be reached by riding the loop.
  • Loops need speed: keep the gas on all the way round. Glowing chevrons are boost strips; cracked amber platforms drop away, so don’t stop on them.
  • Moving traps and laser gates are timed for a bike at a good pace: keep your speed up and they open for you.
  • R restarts, Esc pauses.After a crash, tap to go again.
