How to play
  • One button. Space, the up arrow, or the mouse.Touch anywhere. Hold it on the ground to ride, let go to coast.
  • In the air, holding tips you over backwards. Hold long enough and it's a flip. Let go and the rider steadies the bike.
  • Land on your wheels, close to the slope of the ground. Nearly level is a clean landing: it keeps your combo going and multiplies the next trick.
  • Red and cream with teeth means danger: spikes, the swinging mace, saws, crushers, low beams and laser gates. Gold rounds are coins; a gold ×2 doubles your trick points for a while.
  • Loops need speed: keep holding all the way round. Glowing chevrons are boost strips; cracked amber platforms drop away, so don’t stop on them.
  • Moving traps and laser gates are timed for a bike at a good pace: keep your speed up and they open for you.
  • R restarts, Esc pauses.After a crash, tap to go again.
