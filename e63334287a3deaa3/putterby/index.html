Putterby

Nine toy-town holes of ramps, loops, bumpers, sliding gates, sand and ponds. Drag back from your ball and let go to putt.

How to play
  • Putt: press on or near your ball, drag back the opposite way you want it to go, and let go. The further you drag, the harder the putt. Drag back to the ball to cancel.
  • Camera: drag anywhere else to look around; scroll or pinch to zoom. V or View shows the whole hole. Arrow keys turn and zoom too.
  • Hazards: water and going out of bounds cost a stroke and put you back where you putted from. Sand slows you right down.
  • Loops and ramps need speed: too slow and the ball rolls back down to you.
  • After 7 shots the ball is picked up and you score 8. Lowest total wins.
  • Daily hole: a new hole every day, the same for everyone. Your first finish of the day is your score; play it on more days in a row to build a streak.
  • Online: up to 8 in a room, everyone plays the same hole at once (balls pass through each other). The room's host starts the round.
