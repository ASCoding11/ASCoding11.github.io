  • You run on your own. Sidestep left and right and jump. Every jump is the same big jump, so you can learn exactly how far it goes. You can steer in the air.
  • Walls: run off the side of the floor and you walk up the wall, and the tunnel turns so the wall is your new floor. A wall is often the way round a hole.
  • Tiles: holes are black. White ice is slippery, orange cracked tiles fall away if you land on them, belts push you (yellow sideways, green faster, red slower), lime pads launch you and purple goo slows you down.
  • Levels: reach the ring at the end. Fall and you're straight back at the start. Each level has three crystals.
  • Endless: the tunnel never ends and keeps speeding up. Crystals you pick up are kept.
  • Keys: A / D or the arrow keys sidestep, Space, W or the up arrow jumps, R restarts, Esc pauses. Change them under Controls.
  • Touch: the arrow buttons (or the two halves of the screen, in Settings) sidestep, the green button jumps, and the top-left button pauses.
