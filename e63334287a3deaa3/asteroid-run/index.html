How to play
  • Running is automatic. Sidestep left and right, and jump: a tap is a short hop, holding jump goes higher and further. You can steer in the air.
  • Walls: run off the side of the floor and you walk up the wall, and the whole tunnel turns so the wall is your new floor. When there's a gap, a wall is often the easier way round.
  • Tiles: ice is slippery; cracked tiles hold while you run but give way if you land on them; orange belts push you sideways, green ones speed you up and red ones slow you down; green pads launch you; purple goo is sticky (slow, small jumps).
  • Levels: reach the glowing ring at the end. Falling into space restarts the level, as many times as you like. Each level has three crystals; clearing a level opens the next ones on the map, and some levels open two paths.
  • Endless: the tunnel never ends and slowly gets faster and harder. Crystals you pick up are kept.
  • Runners: new runners unlock as you clear levels and collect crystals.
  • Keys: A / D or the arrow keys sidestep, Space, W or the up arrow jumps, R restarts, Esc pauses. Change them under Controls.
  • Touch: the arrow buttons (or the left and right halves of the screen, in Settings) sidestep, the green button jumps (hold it for a big jump), and the top-left button pauses.
