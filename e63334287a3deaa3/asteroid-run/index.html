Asteroid Run

Run the tunnels at full speed. Jump the holes, or step onto a wall and the whole tunnel turns with you.

  • You run on your own. Sidestep left and right and jump. Tap jump for a small hop, hold it for a big one (or set Jump to Always full). You can steer in the air, and the dark spot under you shows where you'll land.
  • Walls: run off the side of the floor and you walk up the wall, and the tunnel turns so the wall is your new floor. A wall is often the way round a hole.
  • Tiles: holes are black. White ice is slippery, orange cracked tiles fall away if you land on them, belts push you (yellow sideways, green faster, red slower), cyan strips speed you up, lime ramps launch you over big gaps and purple goo slows you down. In the dark, tiles light up as you get near.
  • Levels: reach the ring at the end. Fall and you're straight back at the start. Each level has three crystals. Clear one to open the next; the map splits, and side tunnels each free a new runner.
  • Endless: the tunnel never ends, keeps speeding up and changes zone every few hundred metres. Keep picking up crystals for a streak. Once a run you can spend ◆15 to carry on after a fall. The Daily run is the same tunnel for everyone, all day.
  • Crystals unlock runners early and buy trails.
  • Keys: A / D or the arrow keys sidestep, Space, W or the up arrow jumps, S, the down arrow or Shift is Spool's bridge, R restarts, Esc pauses. Change them under Controls.
  • Touch: the arrow buttons (or the two halves of the screen, in Settings) sidestep, the green button jumps (hold it for a bigger jump), the blue button is Spool's bridge, and the top-left button pauses.

Levels

Clear a level to open the ones it leads to. Each zone teaches one thing; the path splits, and dashed side tunnels are harder and each frees a runner.
