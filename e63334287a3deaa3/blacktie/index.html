Before you play

Black Tie is a spy action game. You fight armed guards of an invented criminal company with guns, fists and explosives; people fall down when they are hit, without blood or gore (a Blood setting exists and is off). Bystanders can't be hurt: they run for cover.

Places are real (Amalfi, on Italy's coast), rebuilt by hand. Every person, company, weapon and name in the story is made up.

Settings

Full locks on and follows; Partial snaps on when you aim, then lets go; Free Aim Assisted slows the crosshair over a target; Free Aim has no help. Flick the camera while locked to switch targets.
