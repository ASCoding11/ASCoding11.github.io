How to play
  • Driving: accelerate, brake (hold it to reverse) and steer. The car turns less the faster you go. To drift, let go of go and tap brake while you turn: the nose swings out and you can take a tight corner fast. Ease off the steering and it grips again.
  • Air: ramps, bumps and crests throw you. In the air, the air brake (or brake) stops the car tumbling and levels it: land straight and you keep your speed, land crooked and you lose it. Orange pads boost you.
  • Runs: R restarts at once, and the clock starts when you press a key. Drive through every checkpoint, in any order, then the finish. Going back to a checkpoint (or falling off) is fine for practice, but that run won't count.
  • Ghosts and boards: your best run drives beside you. Every track has an online top 10, checked by driving each run again; tick up to 10 ghosts to race, and watch any run in the replay viewer.
  • Track builder: snap pieces together, test drive, share the code, or publish it under Community with its own top 10.
  • Keys: W / ↑ go, S / ↓ brake, A D or ← → steer, Space air brake, R or Enter restart, Backspace back to the checkpoint, C camera, M sound, Esc pause. Change them under Controls.
  • Touch: arrows bottom left steer, GO and BRAKE bottom right (BRAKE in the air is the air brake). Top right: back to the checkpoint (flag), restart, camera, sound and pause.
