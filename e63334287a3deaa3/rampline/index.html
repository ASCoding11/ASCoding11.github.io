How to play
  • Driving: accelerate, brake (hold it to reverse) and steer. The car turns less the faster you go, and steering hard at speed makes it slide: ease off and it grips again.
  • Stunts: ramps throw you along the road's angle. Land straight and you keep your speed; land crooked and you lose it. Loops and wall rides hold you on while you're quick enough.
  • Checkpoints: drive through every yellow gate, in any order, and the finish counts. Fall off and you're put back at your last checkpoint, with the clock still running.
  • Medals: bronze, silver, gold and the builder's time on every track. Your best run drives beside you as a see-through ghost.
  • Track builder: snap pieces together on the grid, test drive, and share the code so friends can race your track.
  • Keys: W / ↑ go, S / ↓ brake, A D or ← → steer, R or Enter restart, Backspace back to the checkpoint, C camera, M sound, Esc pause. Change them under Controls.
  • Touch: arrows bottom left steer, GO and BRAKE bottom right. Pause, sound and camera are top right; the pause menu has restart and back to checkpoint.
