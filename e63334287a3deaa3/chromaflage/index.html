    Chromaflage

    Hide and seek where you paint yourself to vanish. Hiders start plain white and copy the room onto their own bodies; seekers get a blaster with limited ink and have to spot them by eye.

    Your name
    Body

    Petite is small but slow to paint big patterns on; Plump hides big furniture-sized areas but is easy to bump into.

    How to play
    1. Hiders get a head start. Open the paint studio, pick colours straight off the walls with the eyedropper, and paint yourself to match a spot.
    2. Strike a pose (crouch under a table, lie flat, squash against a wall) or stick yourself to a wall up high like a poster, then keep still. Moving is the easiest way to get spotted.
    3. Seekers tag hiders with the blaster. Misses cost ink and a short reload, so look before you shoot.
    4. Classic: tagged hiders are out. Infection: tagged hiders join the seekers.
