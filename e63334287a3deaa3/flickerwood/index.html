FLICKERWOOD

▲◀▶▼

Arrows turn · Space dash · P pause

HOW TO PLAY

Steer Glim the firefly and gather every speck of pollen in the glade. Press a turn early and Glim takes it at the next gap.

Swoop flies straight at you.

Gulp leaps for the spot just ahead of you.

Tangle goes for the far side of you from Swoop.

Dither comes close, then loses its nerve.

A moonbloom dazzles them for a few seconds. Touch a dazzled one to send it home: 100, 250, 500, 1000. They flicker before it wears off.

Dash is a short burst of speed, then it has to recharge.

Keep eating without a gap to build a chain, up to 5 times the points.

Grab the find under the stump before it goes. Tunnels at the edges slow the critters, not you.
