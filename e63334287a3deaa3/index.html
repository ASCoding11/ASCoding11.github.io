The Back Room

Four games that aren't on the main site.

Backrooms 1v1

A third-person build-fight duel set in Level 0 of the Backrooms. Shoot, throw up walls and ramps to take height, and eliminate the other player five times before they get you. Every match is a new maze of yellow wallpaper and buzzing lights.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
  • Online 1v1: Quick match pairs you with the next person searching, or pick a room code and send a friend the invite link
  • Rifle, pump shotgun and scoped sniper, with headshots and damage numbers
  • Walls, floors and ramps you can place instantly and shoot down, stacked up to three levels
  • Both players get the same randomly generated maze each match, with flickering lights and a camcorder overlay
  • Practice against the Entity, a bot that hunts you through the maze, boxes up and builds ramps to reach you
▶ Play the game

Needs a keyboard and mouse. Press Quick match, play in a room with a friend, or practice against the bot.

Rumble Karts

A 3D multiplayer kart-battle game that runs in the browser. Grab weapons from item boxes and wreck the other karts in 3-minute rounds, in one of three modes.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
  • Online rooms: share an invite link and friends drop straight into your match
  • Three modes: Classic (most wrecks), Gun Game (level up through all 12 weapons) and Zone (hold the moving zone)
  • 18 items, including a black hole, a charging buffalo and Arjun's Gandiva, a bow with homing arrows
  • An epic item drops onto the map every 30 seconds, and the Gandiva lands with 30 seconds left
  • Three maps that rotate each round: Rumble Yard, slippery Frost Lake and Lava Foundry, where knocking a rival into the lava counts as your wreck
  • Live leaderboard, kill feed, minimap and round results
  • Practice mode against computer-driven karts
  • Keyboard controls on a laptop, touch controls on a phone
▶ Play the game

Pick a room code and press Play online, or try it alone with Practice vs bots.

Starhop

A one-tap space game. Your ship orbits a planet; tap to slingshot off toward the next one. Aim straight at it for a perfect landing, chain perfects for bigger and bigger points, and try not to drift off into space.

  • JavaScript
  • Canvas 2D
  • Web Audio
  • Perfect streaks that climb a musical scale as they grow
  • Drifting planets, tiny planets and rings of spinning mines
  • Eight zones, each with its own colours
  • Missions and shards to unlock eight ships
  • A daily run: the same course for everyone, once a day
▶ Play the game

Single player. Tap, click or press Space. Progress saves in your browser.

Abyssal

A daily trivia game where the obvious answer loses. Each prompt asks you to name something in a category, like a cheese, a dinosaur or a chess opening. The rarer your answer, the deeper your krill sinks toward the trench floor 7,000 m down.

  • JavaScript
  • Canvas 2D
  • Pixel art
  • Daily Dive: seven prompts a day, the same for everyone, with a shareable result and streaks
  • 46 prompts and about 5,200 accepted answers, scored in six tiers from Plankton to Leviathan
  • A Too Clever tier for the "obscure" pick everyone else also thought of
  • Autocorrect for typos, plurals, accents and British spellings, plus "Did you mean…?" for near misses
  • Real depth landmarks on the way down, like the Titanic wreck at 3,800 m
  • Unlimited mode, an archive of past dives, and a Theme Parks pack
▶ Play today's dive

Single player. Type an answer and press Enter. Progress saves in your browser.
