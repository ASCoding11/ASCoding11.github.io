Rumble Karts

A 3D multiplayer kart-battle game that runs in the browser. Grab weapons from item boxes and wreck the other karts in 3-minute rounds, in one of three modes.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Online rooms: share an invite link and friends drop straight into your match
  • Three modes: Classic (most wrecks), Gun Game (level up through all 12 weapons) and Zone (hold the moving zone)
  • 18 items, including a black hole, a charging buffalo and Arjun's Gandiva, a bow with homing arrows
  • An epic item drops onto the map every 30 seconds, and the Gandiva lands with 30 seconds left
  • Three maps that rotate each round: Rumble Yard, slippery Frost Lake and Lava Foundry, where knocking a rival into the lava counts as your wreck
  • Live leaderboard, kill feed, minimap and round results
  • Practice mode against computer-driven karts
  • Keyboard controls on a laptop, touch controls on a phone

Pick a room code and press Play online, or try it alone with Practice vs bots.

Shell Crackers

A first-person free-for-all where everyone is an egg with a gun. Crack the most shells before the five-minute clock runs out. Every hit cracks your shell a little more, and getting cracked leaves a fried egg on the floor.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Online free-for-all for up to 8 eggs: Quick match drops you into a public lobby, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • Five main guns to choose from (a rifle, a pump shotgun, a scoped sniper, an SMG and a burst rifle), plus a pistol, a melee whack and two yolk bombs every life
  • Headshots, damage falloff, aim-down-sights zoom and kill streaks like Hard-Boiled and Uncrackable
  • A ranked rating with a leaderboard: the more real players in a match, the more your rating moves, from ×1 against one real opponent up to ×3 against six or more (bot practice counts ×0.4)
  • Three detailed block-built maps with buildings you can go inside, stairs to upper floors and rooftops, and tunnels underneath: Farmstead (farmhouses with cellars, a barn hayloft, grain towers), Yolk Works (a factory with catwalks, offices and a sewer) and Eggsburg (a hotel, shops, a rooftop bridge, a clock tower and a subway station)
  • Practice against bots that path around the map, strafe and throw yolk bombs, on Easy, Normal or Hard

Plays with a keyboard and mouse, or touch controls on a phone. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or practice against bots.

Backrooms 1v1–4v4

A third-person build-fight shooter set in Level 0 of the Backrooms, for up to eight players on Red and Blue. Shoot, throw up walls and ramps to take height, and wipe out the other team to win the round. Every match is a new maze of yellow wallpaper and buzzing lights.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Online rooms for up to 8: pick Red or Blue in the lobby (or let the host randomize), from 1v1 to 4v4 or uneven teams, first to 3, 5 or 10 rounds
  • Quick match pairs you 1v1 with the next person searching; players who are out watch their teammates until the next round
  • Rifle, pump shotgun and scoped sniper, with headshots and damage numbers
  • Walls, floors and ramps you can place instantly and shoot down, stacked up to three levels
  • Everyone gets the same randomly generated maze each match, bigger for bigger rooms, with flickering lights and a camcorder overlay
  • Practice against the Entity, a bot that hunts you through the maze, boxes up and builds ramps to reach you

Plays with a keyboard and mouse, or touch controls on a phone. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or practice against the bot.

Slipstream

A 3D open-wheel racer with a full 22-car grid. Pick one of 11 teams, wait for the five red lights, then fight for the racing line through braking zones, slipstreams and boosts. Every seat nobody takes is driven by a bot, so every race starts with all 22 cars.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Online rooms for up to 22 drivers: each person picks a team and a seat, and bots fill every empty seat, including your teammate's
  • Quick race drops you into a public lobby, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • If someone leaves mid-race a bot takes over their car, and if the host leaves another player takes over the bots
  • 23 real Grand Prix circuits laid out from map data, from Monaco, Spa and Monza to night races in Singapore and Las Vegas, plus three originals, over 3, 5 or 10 laps
  • A screaming V10 engine note that climbs through the gears, crackles on the overrun and Doppler-shifts as rivals pass
  • Slipstream, an ERS boost that recharges under braking, a live timing tower with gaps, fastest laps and four cameras
  • DRS like F1: from lap 2, get within a second of the car ahead at the detection line and open the rear wing in the zone
  • Keyboard, gamepad (analog stick and triggers) or an analog touch steering pad
  • F1-style track limits: going off deletes your lap, three strikes brings the black-and-white flag, then 5-second penalties; cutting a corner to gain time is a penalty straight away
  • Practice against 21 bots on Easy, Normal or Hard

Plays with a keyboard, a gamepad, or touch controls on a phone. Press Quick race, play in a room with friends, or practice against bots.

Boostball

Rocket-powered car soccer. Drive up the walls, jump, flip into the ball and boost into the air to knock it past the other team's keeper. Five-minute matches, and overtime if it's tied.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Online 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3: Quick match drops you into a public 3v3 lobby, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • Bots fill any empty spots, run by whoever has been in the room longest
  • Rocket League's own physics numbers for jumps, double jumps, flips, flip cancels, half-flips, aerials and air roll, on a full-size field
  • Curved ramps up to the walls: drive up and along the walls, jump off them, and touch the ceiling to get your flip back
  • Powerslides and supersonic demolitions
  • 34 boost pads, ball cam, goal explosions, kickoffs and a scoreboard with goals, assists, saves and shots
  • Three car bodies and ten accent colours
  • Practice against Rookie, Pro or All-Star bots, or warm up alone in free play

Plays with a keyboard, a gamepad or a touch screen. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or practice against bots.

Coil

An online snake arena. Eat glowing pellets to grow, boost to slip in front of other snakes, and when they run into your body their whole length spills out as food for you. Stay inside the red ring.

  • JavaScript
  • Canvas 2D
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Online arenas for up to 10 players: Quick match drops you into the busiest public arena, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • Bots fill every online arena up to 12 snakes, run by whoever has been in the room longest
  • Hold click or Space to boost; boosting burns length and leaves a trail of food behind you
  • A live leaderboard, kill feed, minimap and a personal best saved in your browser
  • 14 skins, from Candy Cane to Spectrum
  • Practice against 12 to 20 bots on Easy, Normal or Hard; Hard bots try to cut you off

Steer with the mouse, keyboard or a finger. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or practice against bots.

Trivium

Trivia with nearly 1,800 questions in 19 categories, from geography and space to money, mythology and New York City, and a fact behind every answer. Play a daily ten alone, or host a contest where everyone answers on their phone.

  • JavaScript
  • Firebase
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Daily ten: the same ten questions for everyone, easy to hard, with a shareable result and streaks
  • Quick play by category and difficulty, Survival with three lives, and a 60-second Blitz
  • Live contests: friends join with a 5-letter code or a QR code, and the host picks categories, difficulty, length and the timer
  • Points for speed and streaks, a breakdown of everyone's answers after each question, live standings and a podium
  • Autopilot runs a contest by itself, and the host can play along from the same screen

Works on a laptop, a projector or a phone. Host a contest from the menu, or join one with a code or an invite link.

Showdown

No-limit Texas Hold'em for up to eight players. Sit down with friends in a private room, or against bots that open by position, c-bet and semi-bluff. Size your bets with the slider, and let the coach tell you whether the price is right.

  • JavaScript
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Crypto
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Online tables for up to 8 players: Quick match seats you at the busiest public table, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • Bots fill the empty seats and step aside when a person sits down
  • Full no-limit rules: blinds and a moving button, minimum raises, short all-ins that don't reopen the betting, side pots, split pots and uncalled bets returned
  • Your hole cards are encrypted to you, so nobody else's browser can read them from the game database
  • If the dealer's browser drops out, the next player takes over and the hand in progress is refunded
  • A coach that shows your hand, your position, how strong your starting hand is, your win chance and the pot odds you need to call
  • Hand history, table chat, standings, a turn timer, check/fold in advance and a four-colour deck
  • Practice against Easy, Normal or Hard bots, heads-up, 6-max or 8-max

Plays with a mouse, a keyboard or a touch screen. Play chips only. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or practice against bots.

Chromaflage

Hide and seek where you paint yourself to disappear. Hiders start plain white and copy the room onto their own bodies, then freeze in a pose. Seekers get a blaster with limited ink and have to spot them by eye.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Online rooms for up to 8 players: Quick match drops you into a public lobby, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • A paint studio: brush, eyedropper that picks colours off anything in the room, fill, undo, a matte-to-glossy finish and a camo check that scores your paint job
  • A soft, rounded Doll or a slim Block body in three sizes, and poses to hide your outline: crouch under tables, lie under beds, squash flat, turn, or stick yourself to a wall up high like a poster
  • Two modes: Classic (tagged hiders are out) and Infection (tagged hiders join the seekers)
  • Misses cost ink and a reload, and the host can make hiders squeak every 20 to 45 seconds
  • Three big indoor maps full of patterns and furniture: Hollyhock Manor, a country house on a party night with a grand staircase; Marlowe Museum, an art museum after closing time; and Kirinoya Inn, a Japanese inn round a garden courtyard
  • Hide practice against a seeker bot that only notices you when your paint doesn't match what's behind you, and Seek practice against bots that paint themselves

Best with a keyboard and mouse; phones get a joystick and buttons. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or practice alone.

Spire

A parkour race up a tower stacked from random sections: beams, spinning bars, vanishing tiles, lifts, conveyors and lasers. There are no checkpoints, so touching anything glowing red sends you back to the bottom. Everyone in the room climbs the same tower against one clock, which runs twice as fast once someone reaches the top.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Online rooms for up to 12 climbers: Quick match drops you into a public tower, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • A new tower every round from 19 hand-built sections, each one checked by a bot that climbs it with the real movement
  • An 8-minute clock (the host can shorten it), finish order with coins and XP, and a height bar showing where everyone is
  • Obby mode: 49 stages over seven themed worlds with checkpoints, and your furthest stage is saved
  • Solo time trials on full or easy towers, and practice for any single section

Plays with a keyboard and mouse (rebindable keys), or touch controls on a phone. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or climb on your own.

Updraft

One way to go, and it's a thousand metres. Climb out of a scrapyard street, over a pile of giant lost things and through the clouds to the edge of space. There are no checkpoints: miss a jump and you fall until something catches you, maybe the street.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
Details
  • One continuous course from a city street to a summit 1000 m up, through five zones that change with the altitude: a tower and its cranes, giant everyday things, floating islands, thin air and the edge of space
  • Standing jumps are short, running jumps go farther and sprint jumps farthest, with a ledge grab for jumps that only just fall short
  • Lifts, swings, drifting clouds, a turning island, fans that lift you, bouncy mushrooms, ice that cracks, and a few launch pads that skip a stretch
  • Your run is saved where you stand, so you can stop and come back; best height, best time and summits are kept
  • Online rooms for up to 12 climbers with a live board of who's highest; after the top, watch the others climb

Best with a keyboard and mouse; phones get a joystick and buttons. Climb solo, press Quick match, or play in a room with friends.

Odd One Out

Props vs Hunters. Props aim at anything in the room and turn into an exact copy of it: a cardboard box, an office chair, a garden gnome. Then they park beside the real ones and keep still. Hunters get a rifle and a few minutes, and every shot at a real object costs them health.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Online rooms for up to 16 players: Quick match drops you into a public lobby, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • More than fifty things to turn into, each copied exactly (shape, colours, size and the way it faces), with lock rotation, turning keys and a line-up button that matches the real ones nearby
  • Matches of 3 to 10 rounds: a hide phase while the hunters wait in a locked room, then the hunt, and everyone takes turns hunting
  • Every prop pings every few seconds (faster in the last minute), so hunters can hear roughly where to look
  • Three maps packed with hundreds of objects: Starlight Galleria, a shopping mall; Willow Lane, a house with a garage and a yard; and Brightside Office
  • Hunt practice against bots that hide by copying nearby objects, and Hide practice against hunter bots that notice movement and things out of place

Best with a keyboard and mouse; phones get a joystick, buttons and drag to look. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or practice alone.

Tumble Trials

A wobbly knockout party show for up to 16 players. Race through obstacle courses before the qualifying spots fill up, survive the sweepers, and be the last bean standing on the vanishing honeycomb to win the crown.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
Details
  • Online rooms for up to 16 players with an invite link, or Quick match into a public lobby; the host picks the show length, bots to fill seats and bumping on or off
  • Shows of two to four rounds: races where only the first few qualify, survivals, then a final for the crown
  • Five races (spinning discs and hammers, tipping seesaws, doors that may be fake, bounce pads and fans, swinging wrecking balls), two survivals and two finals, including three floors of hexagon tiles that vanish after you touch them
  • Run, jump, dive and grab, with bumping, tumbles when something hits you hard, and checkpoints in races
  • A solo show against bots, practice on any level, your own colour, face and pattern, and stats for shows, crowns and rounds qualified

Plays with a keyboard and mouse or a touch screen. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or run a solo show against bots.
