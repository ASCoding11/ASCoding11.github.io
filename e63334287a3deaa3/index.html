The Back Room

Thirteen games that aren't on the main site.

Rumble Karts

A 3D multiplayer kart-battle game that runs in the browser. Grab weapons from item boxes and wreck the other karts in 3-minute rounds, in one of three modes.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
  • Online rooms: share an invite link and friends drop straight into your match
  • Three modes: Classic (most wrecks), Gun Game (level up through all 12 weapons) and Zone (hold the moving zone)
  • 18 items, including a black hole, a charging buffalo and Arjun's Gandiva, a bow with homing arrows
  • An epic item drops onto the map every 30 seconds, and the Gandiva lands with 30 seconds left
  • Three maps that rotate each round: Rumble Yard, slippery Frost Lake and Lava Foundry, where knocking a rival into the lava counts as your wreck
  • Live leaderboard, kill feed, minimap and round results
  • Practice mode against computer-driven karts
  • Keyboard controls on a laptop, touch controls on a phone
▶ Play the game

Pick a room code and press Play online, or try it alone with Practice vs bots.

Shell Crackers

A first-person free-for-all where everyone is an egg with a gun. Crack the most shells before the five-minute clock runs out. Every hit cracks your shell a little more, and getting cracked leaves a fried egg on the floor.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
  • Online free-for-all for up to 8 eggs: Quick match drops you into a public lobby, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • Five main guns to choose from (a rifle, a pump shotgun, a scoped sniper, an SMG and a burst rifle), plus a pistol, a melee whack and two yolk bombs every life
  • Headshots, damage falloff, aim-down-sights zoom and kill streaks like Hard-Boiled and Uncrackable
  • A ranked rating with a leaderboard: the more real players in a match, the more your rating moves, from ×1 against one real opponent up to ×3 against six or more (bot practice counts ×0.4)
  • Two maps that rotate each match: sunny Coop Yard and Sunset Silos
  • Practice against bots that path around the map, strafe and throw yolk bombs, on Easy, Normal or Hard
▶ Play the game

Needs a keyboard and mouse. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or practice against bots.

Abyssal

A daily trivia game where the obvious answer loses. Each prompt asks you to name something in a category, like a cheese, a dinosaur or a chess opening. The rarer your answer, the deeper your krill sinks toward the trench floor 7,000 m down.

  • JavaScript
  • Canvas 2D
  • Pixel art
  • Daily Dive: seven prompts a day, the same for everyone, with a shareable result and streaks
  • 108 prompts and about 15,000 accepted answers, scored in six tiers from Plankton to Leviathan
  • A Too Clever tier for the "obscure" pick everyone else also thought of
  • Autocorrect for typos, plurals, accents and British spellings, plus "Did you mean…?" for near misses
  • Real depth landmarks on the way down, like the Titanic wreck at 3,800 m
  • Unlimited mode, an archive of past dives, and eight themed packs: Theme Parks, Movies & TV, Sports, Food & Drink, Science & Math, Around the World, New York City and Pop Culture
▶ Play today's dive

Single player. Type an answer and press Enter. Progress saves in your browser.

Starhop

A one-tap space game. Your ship orbits a planet; tap to slingshot off toward the next one. Aim straight at it for a perfect landing, chain perfects for bigger and bigger points, and try not to drift off into space.

  • JavaScript
  • Canvas 2D
  • Web Audio
  • Perfect streaks that climb a musical scale as they grow
  • Drifting planets, tiny planets and rings of spinning mines
  • Eight zones, each with its own colours
  • Missions and shards to unlock eight ships
  • A daily run: the same course for everyone, once a day
▶ Play the game

Single player. Tap, click or press Space. Progress saves in your browser.

Backrooms 1v1

A third-person build-fight duel set in Level 0 of the Backrooms. Shoot, throw up walls and ramps to take height, and eliminate the other player five times before they get you. Every match is a new maze of yellow wallpaper and buzzing lights.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
  • Online 1v1: Quick match pairs you with the next person searching, or pick a room code and send a friend the invite link
  • Rifle, pump shotgun and scoped sniper, with headshots and damage numbers
  • Walls, floors and ramps you can place instantly and shoot down, stacked up to three levels
  • Both players get the same randomly generated maze each match, with flickering lights and a camcorder overlay
  • Practice against the Entity, a bot that hunts you through the maze, boxes up and builds ramps to reach you
▶ Play the game

Needs a keyboard and mouse. Press Quick match, play in a room with a friend, or practice against the bot.

Slipstream

A 3D open-wheel racer with a full 22-car grid. Pick one of 11 teams, wait for the five red lights, then fight for the racing line through braking zones, slipstreams and boosts. Every seat nobody takes is driven by a bot, so every race starts with all 22 cars.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
  • Online rooms for up to 22 drivers: each person picks a team and a seat, and bots fill every empty seat, including your teammate's
  • Quick race drops you into a public lobby, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • If someone leaves mid-race a bot takes over their car, and if the host leaves another player takes over the bots
  • 23 real Grand Prix circuits laid out from map data, from Monaco, Spa and Monza to night races in Singapore and Las Vegas, plus three originals, over 3, 5 or 10 laps
  • A screaming V10 engine note that climbs through the gears, crackles on the overrun and Doppler-shifts as rivals pass
  • Slipstream, an ERS boost that recharges under braking, a live timing tower with gaps, fastest laps and four cameras
  • DRS like F1: from lap 2, get within a second of the car ahead at the detection line and open the rear wing in the zone
  • Keyboard, gamepad (analog stick and triggers) or an analog touch steering pad
  • F1-style track limits: going off deletes your lap, three strikes brings the black-and-white flag, then 5-second penalties; cutting a corner to gain time is a penalty straight away
  • Practice against 21 bots on Easy, Normal or Hard
▶ Play the game

Plays with a keyboard, a gamepad, or touch controls on a phone. Press Quick race, play in a room with friends, or practice against bots.

Boostball

Rocket-powered car soccer. Drive up the walls, jump, flip into the ball and boost into the air to knock it past the other team's keeper. Five-minute matches, and overtime if it's tied.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
  • Online 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3: Quick match drops you into a public 3v3 lobby, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • Bots fill any empty spots, run by whoever has been in the room longest
  • Rocket League's own physics numbers for jumps, double jumps, flips, flip cancels, half-flips, aerials and air roll, on a full-size field
  • Curved ramps up to the walls: drive up and along the walls, jump off them, and touch the ceiling to get your flip back
  • Powerslides and supersonic demolitions
  • 34 boost pads, ball cam, goal explosions, kickoffs and a scoreboard with goals, assists, saves and shots
  • Three car bodies and ten accent colours
  • Practice against Rookie, Pro or All-Star bots, or warm up alone in free play
▶ Play the game

Plays with a keyboard, a gamepad or a touch screen. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or practice against bots.

Kartwheel

A 3D kart racer with eight cartoon animal racers, drawn like a 1996 console game. Hop into a power slide and wiggle the stick until the tyre smoke turns from grey to yellow to red, let go for a mini-turbo, smash item gems for something to throw, and race for the cup over four tracks.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
  • Power slides like the 1996 original (wiggle out of the turn and back until the smoke goes red), rocket starts on the countdown, boost pads, and ramps where a trick in the air gives a boost on landing
  • Items: Turbo Peppers (one or three), Oil Slicks, Bumper Balls, Homing Hornets, the Comet that hunts down the leader, Bubble Shields, Mud Splats and the Supernova. The further back you are, the better your item
  • Four tracks: Meadow Loop, Coral Cove, Ember Peak with lava on the hairpins and a jump over the flow, and Nebula Run, which has no walls
  • A four-race Grand Prix with points and a trophy, or single races at 50cc, 100cc or 150cc
  • Online rooms for up to 8 racers: pick a racer, and bots drive every kart nobody picked. Quick race drops you into a public lobby, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • N64-style graphics: a 240-line picture with 16-bit colour, chunky blurred textures, thick fog and pre-rendered kart sprites. A Modern setting switches to clean full-resolution 3D
  • Eight racers in three weight classes, a synth soundtrack for each track, and keyboard, gamepad or touch controls
▶ Play the game

Plays with a keyboard, a gamepad, or touch buttons on a phone. Racers, items and tracks are all original.

Coil

An online snake arena. Eat glowing pellets to grow, boost to slip in front of other snakes, and when they run into your body their whole length spills out as food for you. Stay inside the red ring.

  • JavaScript
  • Canvas 2D
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
  • Online arenas for up to 10 players: Quick match drops you into the busiest public arena, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • Bots fill every online arena up to 12 snakes, run by whoever has been in the room longest
  • Hold click or Space to boost; boosting burns length and leaves a trail of food behind you
  • A live leaderboard, kill feed, minimap and a personal best saved in your browser
  • 14 skins, from Candy Cane to Spectrum
  • Practice against 12 to 20 bots on Easy, Normal or Hard; Hard bots try to cut you off
▶ Play the game

Steer with the mouse, keyboard or a finger. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or practice against bots.

Quarry

A block sandbox that plays like the classic survival games. Punch a tree, turn the logs into planks, then lay out a pickaxe in a crafting table's 3×3 grid. Smelt iron in a furnace, cook meat to keep your hunger up, and have somewhere lit before dark, when the zombies come out. When you're ready, build a portal to the Nether, and from there find the way to the End.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • WebGL shaders
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
  • Shared online worlds for up to 8 players: Quick join a public world, or pick a world code and send friends the invite link. Everything built there stays, including what's in chests and furnaces
  • Real crafting: a 2×2 grid in your inventory, a 3×3 grid at the crafting table, shaped recipes for 25 tools in five materials, and a recipe book that lays recipes out for you
  • Furnaces that smelt over time on coal or wood, chests, dropped items you walk over to pick up, and the inventory controls you'd expect: drag to split stacks, shift-click, number-key swaps, Q to throw
  • Huge caves to explore: open caverns, long winding tunnels, deep ravines that crack open the surface and lava lakes at the bottom, with dark deepslate further down
  • Every Overworld ore: coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, lapis lazuli, diamonds and, up in the mountains, emeralds. Each one has a deepslate version, shows up at its own depths and can be crafted into a block of it
  • The Nether and the End: light an obsidian frame with flint and steel to cross into lava seas, crimson and warped forests and nether brick fortresses. Then follow Seeker eyes to a stronghold, open its End portal and take on the Void Wyrm among its crystal towers
  • Water and lava that really flow: they spread out in levels, run downhill toward the nearest drop, pour off ledges as waterfalls and carry you along. Two water sources make a third, and water meeting lava hardens into cobblestone or obsidian. Scoop them up with buckets
  • Hunger, eating, fall damage, drowning and lava; die and everything you carried spills where you fell
  • Pigs, cows, sheep and hens to farm by day, and zombies in the dark that burn up at sunrise. Sleep through the night in a bed (everyone online has to be in bed) and wake up there after you die
  • Farming: hoe grass into farmland, keep it near water, and grow wheat from seeds into bread. Saplings from leaves grow into trees, and the hills are covered in tall grass and flowers
  • Redstone: dust wires that carry power 15 blocks, levers, buttons, pressure plates, inverting torches, repeaters, lamps, and oak and iron doors that open on a signal
  • Arjunity, an optional mod: a floating helper drone who answers your questions in chat and out loud in an Indian English voice (where the diamonds are, recipes, maths, the time). Set him to Spooky and, after a few nights, he starts watching you from the dark
  • Creative mode with every item, a debug screen, third-person camera, chat with friends and commands like /time and /give in your own worlds
▶ Play the game

Needs a keyboard and mouse. Make a world on your own device, Quick join a public world, or build with friends in a world of your own.

Hail Mary

Retro pixel football, one on one. Call a play, drop back and drop the pass in before the rush gets home, then line up on defense and try to stop your friend. Four quarters, a running clock and a scoreboard to match.

  • JavaScript
  • Canvas 2D
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Audio
  • Online 1v1: Quick match pairs you with the next person searching, or pick a room code and send a friend the invite link
  • Six plays on offense, from Four Verts to a Toss Sweep, against four coverages on defense, from Cover 3 to an all-out blitz
  • Hold to aim a pass and let go to throw, then juke and dive for extra yards with the ball carrier
  • On defense you steer one player and switch to whoever is nearest the ball; let go and he plays his assignment
  • First downs, sacks, interceptions and pick sixes, punts, field goals on a kick meter, two-point tries and sudden-death overtime
  • Pick your team name and colours, and practice against the CPU on Easy, Normal or Hard
▶ Play the game

Plays with a mouse and keyboard or on a touch screen. Press Quick match, play in a room with a friend, or practice against the CPU.

Trivium

Trivia with nearly 1,800 questions in 19 categories, from geography and space to money, mythology and New York City, and a fact behind every answer. Play a daily ten alone, or host a contest where everyone answers on their phone.

  • JavaScript
  • Firebase
  • Web Audio
  • Daily ten: the same ten questions for everyone, easy to hard, with a shareable result and streaks
  • Quick play by category and difficulty, Survival with three lives, and a 60-second Blitz
  • Live contests: friends join with a 5-letter code or a QR code, and the host picks categories, difficulty, length and the timer
  • Points for speed and streaks, a breakdown of everyone's answers after each question, live standings and a podium
  • Autopilot runs a contest by itself, and the host can play along from the same screen
▶ Play the game

Works on a laptop, a projector or a phone. Host a contest from the menu, or join one with a code or an invite link.

Showdown

No-limit Texas Hold'em for up to eight players. Sit down with friends in a private room, or against bots that open by position, c-bet and semi-bluff. Size your bets with the slider, and let the coach tell you whether the price is right.

  • JavaScript
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Web Crypto
  • Web Audio
  • Online tables for up to 8 players: Quick match seats you at the busiest public table, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • Bots fill the empty seats and step aside when a person sits down
  • Full no-limit rules: blinds and a moving button, minimum raises, short all-ins that don't reopen the betting, side pots, split pots and uncalled bets returned
  • Your hole cards are encrypted to you, so nobody else's browser can read them from the game database
  • If the dealer's browser drops out, the next player takes over and the hand in progress is refunded
  • A coach that shows your hand, your position, how strong your starting hand is, your win chance and the pot odds you need to call
  • Hand history, table chat, standings, a turn timer, check/fold in advance and a four-colour deck
  • Practice against Easy, Normal or Hard bots, heads-up, 6-max or 8-max
▶ Play the game

Plays with a mouse, a keyboard or a touch screen. Play chips only. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or practice against bots.
