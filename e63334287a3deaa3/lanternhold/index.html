How to play
  • The Gloom walks the road to your lantern. Each one that reaches it costs lives (big ones cost more, a boss costs 15).
  • Pick a tower below (or press 1 to 6) and click a grass tile.Pick a tower below, tap a grass tile to see it there, then tap it again to build. Click or tap a tower to upgrade it twice, change what it aims at, or sell it for 70% back.
  • Flyers (Duskmoths) skip the road and fly straight at the lantern (on Whorl Garden, from their own pink gate). Thumpers can't hit them; Sky Lances only hit them.
  • Shellbacks are armoured: small hits barely scratch them. Thumpers and Sun Lenses (which ignore armour) do the job.
  • Every 10th wave brings the Gloomwarden. Survive 30 waves to win, then keep going in endless mode.
  • Send a wave early for bonus gold. Drag or right-drag to move the view, scroll to zoom. Space sends the wave, F changes speed.Drag to move the view, pinch to zoom.
  • With friends, the team's gold is shared out evenly: every kill and wave bonus is split between everyone, and each player spends their own share. Give gold to a teammate from the team list.
