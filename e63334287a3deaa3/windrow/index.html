Windrow

The old gardens are buried in autumn. Gather, rake and blow every last leaf away, one walled garden at a time.

Your garden saves itself in this browser.

Paused

Brown dots on the map are leaves still on the ground. Red flags are compost bins, yellow squares are mulch chutes.

Settings

Changing detail redraws the leaves that are left, in new places. What you have cleared and earned stays the same.

How to play

  1. Every leaf is real. Nothing vanishes until it is in a compost bin or down a mulch chute.
  2. Hands: hold to gather the leaves in the ring into your sack, then empty the sack at a bin with a red flag.
  3. Rake: each stroke drags leaves toward you. Pull them into heaps, or straight over a yellow grate.
  4. Blower: pushes leaves away in a cone, strongest close up and in the middle. Walls stop the wind and catch the leaves.
  5. Acorns pay for bigger and better tools in the shed, and open the next garden gate.
  6. Clear a garden completely for a bonus and a new trick. The leaf finder points at the last few.
