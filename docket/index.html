Study planner for Canvas

Docket

Pull in your Canvas assignments and your calendar. Docket sizes up how hard each assignment is and how long it'll take, then fits the work around your classes, practices and everything else you do.

Get started

01Bring in your work

One-click Canvas import

A bookmark button reads your upcoming assignments straight from Canvas while you're logged in, including instructions, points and whether you've already turned it in.

Send to Docket
  1. Drag the green button onto your bookmarks bar. (No bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B, or ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac.)
  2. Open Canvas and log in, on any page.
  3. Click the bookmark. Docket opens in a new tab with the next six weeks of work. Click it again any time to refresh.

Or use your Canvas calendar feed

In Canvas, open Calendar, then click Calendar Feed at the bottom right and copy the link. It lists every assignment with a due date.

If your school blocks the link from loading here, open it in a new tab to download the .ics file, then drop that file in the Calendar tab.

Add your calendar

Docket blocks off everything already on your calendar (classes, games, appointments) and only plans study time in the gaps.

Where to get the file

  1. Google Calendar: Settings → Import & export → Export. That downloads a .zip you can drop here as is.
  2. Apple Calendar: select the calendar, then File → Export → Export….
  3. Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish, then open the ICS link to download it.
  4. Canvas: the Calendar Feed link (see the Canvas tab) also downloads as an .ics file.

    Paste your Canvas assignments page

    Open a course's Assignments page (or your To Do list), select all with Ctrl+A or ⌘+A, copy, and paste it here. Docket finds the titles, due dates and points.

    Add something that isn't on Canvas

    A paper handout, a college essay, studying for a test. Leave the time blank and Docket estimates it.

    Everything stays in this browser. Your assignments and calendar are never uploaded anywhere, and Docket can only read Canvas, never submit or change anything.
