Privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Movi is a free app for ranking the movies you've seen and sharing them with friends. We collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your information, and there are no ads or tracking.

Who this covers

This policy covers Movi at ngcoding10.github.io/claude and the same app at ascoding11.github.io/movi. Both addresses use the same accounts and the same data. "We" means the person who runs Movi.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, a username, a display name, and optionally a short bio, an avatar color and a yearly goal. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • What you do in Movi: your rankings and scores, notes you write about films, your watchlist and To rank list, who you follow and who follows you, posts, likes, comments, recommendations you send or receive, and notifications.
  • Lists of titles you type into To rank, which are used to find the matching films.
  • Settings saved in your browser: whether you've finished the welcome steps, films you've skipped, and which suggestions you've seen recently, so they don't repeat. These stay on your device.

We don't collect your location, contacts, payment details or browsing history, and Movi has no ads, analytics or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To show your lists, compute your scores, and suggest films you might like.
  • To show your activity to the people who can see your profile, and theirs to you.
  • To send emails you ask for, such as a password reset.

Who can see what

Your username, display name, bio, avatar, rankings, posts and follower counts are visible to other Movi members and on your public profile. If you make your account private in Settings, only people you approve as followers see your rankings and activity. Your email address is never shown to other members.

Services we use

We don't sell or rent your information. Movi relies on a few services to work, and they handle data only to provide that service:

  • Supabase stores accounts and app data and handles sign-in.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" if you use it. Google Fonts serves the app's fonts.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives the movie titles you type into To rank, with no name or email attached, to match them to real films.
  • GitHub Pages (and Cloudflare, for ascoding11.com) hosts the website, and jsDelivr serves a code library. Like any web host, they may log basic request data such as IP addresses.
  • TMDB serves the movie posters. Your browser loads them directly from TMDB.

We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, rankings, lists, posts, comments, likes and follows are deleted.

Your choices

  • See and download your data: Settings → Your data → Export my data.
  • Change your details: edit your profile or make it private in Settings.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account. This can't be undone.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the database only lets each member change their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Children

Movi is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For big changes, we'll also say so in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Open an issue at github.com/ngcoding10/claude/issues.
