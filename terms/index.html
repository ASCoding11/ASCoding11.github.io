Ages

The site is made for students. Anyone can play the games and use the study tools without an account. If you're under 13, please don't sign in, don't post on the Marketplace and don't buy anything. If you're under 18, ask a parent or guardian before you buy, sell or make a payment of any kind.

Playing fair and being kind

  • Pick player names, Aisle names and messages that you'd be happy for anyone to read. No bullying, hate, threats, sexual content, or sharing anyone's personal details.
  • Don't cheat in ways that spoil the game for others: no editing scores or saved data you send to the site, no bots or scripts that play for you, and no using bugs to hurt other players. If you find a bug, please tell us instead.
  • Don't try to break, overload or get around the site's limits, its database or its servers, and don't try to get into anyone else's account or data.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else, including Arjun or the site's admins.

Names and messages go through a filter, and players can report chat. The owner and admins can hide or delete names, messages, listings and scores, and block anyone from online features, if they think the rules were broken.
