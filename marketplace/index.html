Post or browse

Sign in with Google, add a photo and a price. Listings show the item and a rough area, never your name.

02

Haggle

Each buyer gets a private thread with the seller: offer, counter, accept. The best open offer shows on the listing.

03

Pay, then meet

The buyer pays the agreed price by card through Stripe. Only then do you both get each other's name and contact details.

Rules and fees

Listing is free and the marketplace takes no cut of your sale. Stripe's card processing fee (2.9% + 30¢) comes out of the seller's payout.

Sell only things that are yours and legal for anyone to buy. No weapons, alcohol, vapes, drugs or medicine, no tickets, accounts, schoolwork or answers, and nothing stolen or counterfeit. Listings that get reported are hidden and can be removed.

Keep phone numbers, emails, links and usernames out of listings and offers: you get each other's details when the item is paid for. Meet in a public place, bring someone with you, and check the item before you leave. If you're under 18, ask a parent or guardian before buying or selling, and have them set up payouts with you. Sales are between the buyer and the seller; if an item never turns up, use the report button so the listing can be looked at.
