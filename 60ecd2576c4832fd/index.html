Crush Hour

A dating sim set over one week in New York. Meet four people on Monday, spend five evenings getting to know them (or yourself), and on Sunday ask one of them to ride the Wonder Wheel at Coney Island. Then there's Gary.

  • JavaScript
  • SVG
  • Four people to meet: Juno the pastry chef, Theo the jazz bassist who fixes subway signals, Priya the urban planner and Sam the park ranger
  • Three dates each, all over the city, from a basement jazz club to the Brooklyn Bridge at sunrise, with choices that change how they feel about you
  • Wit, Nerve and Heart: build them up at open mics, the library or on the phone with your mom to unlock bolder answers
  • Fourteen endings, including one for people who spend too much time feeding pigeons
  • An endings gallery, and your week saves at the start of each evening
▶ Play the game

Single player, about 15 minutes a week. Click, tap or press Space to continue; 1, 2 and 3 pick an answer.
